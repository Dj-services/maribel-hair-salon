About the Salon

A Welcoming Salon Experience

At Maribel Beauty Salon, every visit starts with a warm hello. Our neighborhood salon on Washington Street is built around one simple idea: you should leave feeling comfortable, cared for, and confident in your hair.

Clients describe the salon as a friendly, relaxed space where their stylist takes the time to listen — whether that means a fresh haircut, a color refresh, or a treatment tailored to their hair's needs.

It's the kind of place where you're greeted by name, not just a number on a schedule — the welcoming experience our neighbors write about again and again.
